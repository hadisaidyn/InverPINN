## Mechanistic hypothesis

The field can match sparse observations without being the exact PDE solution
of its simultaneously inferred source. This permits low Q and altered field
derivatives to compensate in a finite weighted objective. The evidence supports
this mismatch, but does **not** establish simultaneous updates as its sole cause.

At fixed neural parameters, sensor loss has zero derivative with respect to
source x, y and Q. This was verified in all logged direct data-source gradients.
The source receives observations indirectly through the field's PDE gradients.
For J0, data loss decreased in 1,653 logged intervals; in 487 (29.46%), Q
simultaneously moved away from the observation-optimal amplitude at the **final
estimated location**. That target is not the instantaneous optimum at each
intermediate location. The count is descriptive, not a causal effect estimate.

The median of per-case median data/PDE field-gradient cosines was +0.526 for
J0, -0.174 for J1 and -0.027 for J2. Thus the evidence does not support a claim
of universally conflicting J0 gradients. Raw-location and field gradient norms,
Q update sizes, losses and parameter trajectories are preserved for every run.

Post-fit consistency checks compare neural readings with independent 201-square
forward readings generated by the **same inferred source**:

| Candidate | Median neural sensor RMSE | Median forward sensor RMSE | Median neural/forward discrepancy, relative to observed norm | Forward fit worse, /24 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| J0 | 0.000564 | 0.001773 | 3.342% | 22 |

These explanatory checks include inference-solver discretization error; they

The median PDE-profiled-Q minus observation-profiled-Q gap at the final field

J0's median distance to its PDE-optimal Q was only 0.004749, versus 0.017919
to the observation-optimal Q. Optimizing the PDE amplitude exactly therefore
need not solve the observation inverse problem.

## Mathematical intervention

The pre-fit [derivation](source_field_coupling.md) specifies the actual objective:

\[
L=\frac{10}{C_*^2}\operatorname{mean}(C_\theta-z)^2
 +\frac{T_*^2}{C_*^2}\operatorname{mean}(R_0-QG)^2
 +\frac{L_{IC}+L_{BC}}{C_*^2},\qquad
R_0=C_t+uC_x+vC_y-D(C_{xx}+C_{yy}),
\]

where C*=0.88, T*=0.8 and G is the unit-peak Gaussian with sigma=0.08.
Q is peak intensity (concentration/time), not integrated emitted mass.
For positive fixed weights a, H=sum(a G²)>0,

\[
Q^*=\max\left(\epsilon,\frac{\sum_i a_iG_iR_{0,i}}{\sum_i a_iG_i^2}\right).
\]

The actual batches have a=1; the common PDE weight cancels. Epsilon is the
dtype's smallest positive normal value, explicitly resolving the open-domain
Q>0 infimum when the unconstrained optimum is nonpositive. Float64 accumulation
is followed by conversion to the field dtype. Degenerate/nonfinite inputs fail.
Tests compare the analytical formula with scalar numerical minimization,
including agreement within 2e-14 with analytic-Jacobian least squares in float64.

At an interior optimum, differentiable projection obeys the envelope theorem;
its gradient equals that from a detached exact optimum. The profiled objective
is `<R0,R0> - <G,R0>²/<G,G>` with the same normalized inner product. Removing
the Q optimization coordinate does not remove field freedom along G or add a
direct observation-to-source loss. This is a limitation of J1, not a bug in
the least-squares formula.

| Candidate | Fixed procedure |
|---|---|
| J0 | Unchanged B_revised, joint Adam for 12,000 updates. |
| J1 | Field and sigmoid x/y updated jointly for 12,000 updates; differentiable PDE-Q profile each step; Q is a buffer, not an Adam parameter. |
| J2 | 60 cycles: 150 field-only updates with source fixed, then 50 x/y-only updates with field fixed and Q profiled. Separate persistent Adam states. |
| J3 | **PINN-location + forward-profiled-Q**: unchanged J0 x/y, observation-only unit-source forward solve at 201², Q=hᵀz/hᵀh, and numerical field reconstruction. |

J1/J2 end with one predeclared Q-only profile on 8,192 separately seeded fitting
points. Their terminal Q values need not equal the last minibatch profile;
plots mark this final operation. All neural fields retain the same 64-wide,
three-hidden-layer tanh MLP and
`C=16x(1-x)y(1-y)(t/0.8) softplus(f_theta)` constraint. Adam lr=0.001,
initial x=y=0.5 and Q=1.1, batch sizes, source width and loss weights are fixed.
J2 has 9,000 field and 3,000 location updates: equal total updates, **not**
equal field-update count or wall time. This confounds an isolated causal
comparison of simultaneity versus alternation.

J4 was not run: J2 already implements the proposed combined field/location/Q
sequence and field-frozen location-only Adam. No distinct fourth intervention
was preregistered; the results do not justify an additional rescue search.

## Interpretation

The results demonstrate imperfect consistency between the learned neural field
and inferred source. They do not prove that simultaneous updates alone cause
the bias. J1 helps while retaining joint field/location updates. J2 reduces the
trajectory away fraction to286/1703=16.79%, yet makes bias and recovery worse;
this trajectory count is therefore not a sufficient compensation endpoint.
The PDE/observation Q gap also worsens to -0.018584. Alternating this schedule
does not solve the problem, and its smaller field budget limits causal attribution.

J1 meets every absolute promising target: median location<=.02, median Q<=10%,
non-universal underestimation, median L2<=15%, zero negativity, exact BC/IC,
and no material PDE median/p90 regression. It also meets distributional and
seed-recovery noninferiority. However:

- Median relative Q error improvement is **19.828673%**, below the frozen20%.
- Absolute mean signed-Q bias improvement is **19.436501%**, below20%.

Neither gate is rounded into a pass. Their closeness is not a physical
discontinuity, but changing them after observing results would invalidate the
prospective selection. J2 fails multiple source/field/physics/reliability gates.
J3 is a hybrid, was ineligible, and has worse median L2. Consequently
**B_revised_2 is not created**. The current pure PINN formulation remains
inadequate for a reliable-source-recovery claim despite J1's development gains.

One final fresh blind benchmark is not justified under this milestone's
selection rule. No benchmark is scheduled or run. If a later, separately
authorized development stage qualifies a frozen model, robustness testing
should require a prospectively specified successful blind result: at least
90% joint recovery (27/30 for30 cases), the location<=.08/Q<=20% per-case
criterion, the present median source/field targets, exact constraints and no
material PDE regression. This is a proposed future advancement requirement,
not a redefinition of any completed benchmark. No claim about real pollution,
unknown weather, sensor noise, arbitrary sources or Almaty follows here.

## Limitation

Only24 stratified, noiseless, correctly specified single-source development
cases were used. Selection is not independent confirmation. Worst cases remain
severe; no outliers were discarded. One reference misses the estimated1% target.
J1 minibatch projection can be noisy and does not impose exact PDE solutions.
J2 has fewer field updates and a single fixed schedule, not an exhaustive test
of alternation. Four repeated-seed cases are insufficient for broad optimizer
robustness claims. The hard field envelope, penalty weights, representation
capacity and residual sampling remain possible interacting explanations.
J3's discretization and PDE diagnostic differ from neural evaluation.
